Filmography

Welles is credited as the director of 16 feature films and documentaries, although two were released posthumously. He is credited as the writer of 13 of those films, and starred in over 60 total films. This abridged filmography chronicles his most notable and lasting works:

  • Citizen Kane (1941) -- Director, Co-Writer, Star
  • The Magnificent Ambersons (1942) -- Director, Writer
  • The Stranger (1946) -- Director, Star
  • The Lady from Shanghai (1947) -- Director, Writer, Star
  • Macbeth (1948) -- Director, Star
  • The Third Man (1949) -- Star
  • Othello (1951) -- Director, Writer, Star
  • Touch of Evil (1958) -- Director, Writer, Star
  • The Trial (1962) -- Director, Writer, Star
  • Chimes at Midnight (1965) -- Director, Writer, Star
  • F for Fake (1973) -- Director, Writer, Star
  • The Other Side of the Wind (filmed in 1985, pothumously released in 2018) -- Director, Writer, Star
