September 11, 2001 • New York City

Key moments of the World Trade Center attack

An interactive, non-graphic timeline of the morning attacks and the collapse of the World Trade Center towers. Times are approximate Eastern Daylight Time (EDT).

8:46 a.m.American Airlines Flight 11 strikes the North Tower
9:03 a.m.United Airlines Flight 175 strikes the South Tower
9:59 a.m.South Tower collapses
10:28 a.m.North Tower collapses

American Airlines Flight 11, a Boeing 767, departs Boston and is later hijacked. It is diverted toward New York City.

Aviation

Flight 11 crashes into the North Tower (1 World Trade Center), beginning the attacks in New York.

Impact

United Airlines Flight 175, also a Boeing 767, crashes into the South Tower. The second impact is witnessed live by television audiences around the world.

Impact

American Airlines Flight 77 crashes into the Pentagon, the headquarters of the U.S. Department of Defense.

Nationwide attacks

The South Tower collapses, less than an hour after it was struck. Thousands of people had been evacuating the complex and surrounding area.

Collapse

United Airlines Flight 93 crashes in Somerset County after passengers and crew attempt to regain control of the aircraft. Its intended target is not reached.

Flight 93

The North Tower collapses. The collapse follows the earlier destruction of the South Tower and marks the loss of both World Trade Center towers.

Collapse

7 World Trade Center, a 47-story building north of the main complex, collapses after sustaining severe damage and prolonged fires.
